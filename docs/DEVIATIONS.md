# Deviations from the pre-registration and the runbook

Every departure from `docs/PREREGISTRATION.md` (after it is frozen) or from the runbook is recorded here before or at the time it happens, never afterwards. A deviation entry has: date, what was planned, what was done instead, why, and which results it affects. Entries before the v1 freeze record departures from the runbook and from the v0 draft; they are exploratory decisions and are listed so that the v1 document does not hide them.

## Entries

### 2026-09-04, model fallback list

- Planned: Qwen3-1.7B as the fallback model if the Qwen3.5 stack check failed.
- Done: the Qwen3-1.7B stack check failed and the Qwen3.5-2B check passed; the fallback list is closed and Qwen3.5-2B is the primary model.
- Affects: nothing pre-registered.

### 2026-09-05, calibration runs relaunched after CUDA OOM, and the detached launch protocol

- Planned: one calibration run per model, launched with `modal run`.
- Done: both first attempts failed with CUDA OOM (policy loaded in fp32 by the trainer default, 4-sequence log-prob chunk); relaunched after loading in bf16 with a 1-sequence chunk and a micro-batch of 2. The relaunch was cut off when the launching laptop lost its network and Modal cancelled the client's inputs; the 4B run was lost, the 2B run had committed its volume seconds earlier. Runs are now deployed once per commit and spawned detached (`scripts/launch_modal.py`), collected at any time (`scripts/collect_modal.py`), and retried by the platform on preemption with the previous attempt's evidence preserved.
- Affects: cost only (the failed attempts are in the ledger).

### 2026-09-05, environment v1 to v2 after the P12 miss

- Planned (runbook): freeze the environment after a single calibration.
- Done: the v1 calibration gave lambda = 0.030 (P12 failed) and exposed three defects (numeric postal codes rejected, saturated diagnostic tasks, benign fault mix keyed by call index). Environment v2 (request-keyed faults, outage type, budget 7 + 2n, task mix, diagnostic task rule) was built and re-calibrated (lambda = 0.222 for 2B). This is exploratory calibration; the v1 negative result stays in `registers/calibration_env_v1.json` and is reported in the paper.
- Affects: the noise mixture is a design choice informed by the model's zero-shot behavior; the paper says so and the outage-free condition C2r tests the mixture that was not tuned.

### 2026-09-06, environment v3 and protocol fixes after the external review (before the freeze)

- Found: (1) the four evaluation schedule seeds were shared by every task and never flipped at q = 0.05 or 0.10, so the v0 outcome-noise evaluation applied no outcome corruption; (2) fault events were keyed by all tool arguments including free text, so rewording a cancellation reason dodged a persistent outage (reproduced on heldout-00077, seed 0); (3) luck-share tables merged diagnostic checkpoints; (4) training episodes were stamped without step or attempt, and no training-group register existed; (5) trainer checkpoints were disabled, so a retry restarted from scratch and overwrote the previous attempt's logs; (6) `train_only` still ran the periodic evaluations; (7) the loss type in the v0 document (grpo) did not match the implemented default (dapo); (8) the validation pool had informed calibration and no separate test pool existed.
- Done: per-task evaluation schedules (`eval_schedule_seed`); fault events keyed by tool and resource (`fault_key`), with metamorphic tests; per-checkpoint luck-share tables with exact cardinality checks; phase and attempt stamps, event keys on every logged call, and the training-group register `groups.jsonl`; trainer checkpoints every 20 steps with resume and attempt directories; `train_only` honored; the document now states dapo; a test pool of 300 tasks generated after calibration and reserved for the final evaluation; the scripted-reference register rebuilt on both pools under the frozen evaluation schedules; the outage-free mixture C2r and the matched challenge set added; the theory notes corrected (THEORY.md section 3b).
- Affects: every number measured before 2026-09-06 (v1 and v2 calibrations, speed checks, and any gate-1 run launched at commit bd2bf4e) was produced under the old keying and the old evaluation seeds; they remain in their registers as exploratory measurements and are not compared with v3 numbers. The zero-shot levels are re-measured under v3 (`calib-v3-*`) before the freeze.

### 2026-09-06, run matrix and budget

- Planned (runbook): 27 pre-registered runs including a 9B scale check and a flaky-test census, at an assumed 1.5 hours per run.
- Done: measured generation speed (230 to 336 s per batch of 128 episodes, independent of prefix caching and batched-token limits) gives about 17 hours per 100-step run; the matrix is tiered and the funded list is set at the freeze from the gate-1 measured step time; 9B and the census are dropped, 4B is last.
- Affects: which conditions are confirmatory (tier 1) and which are "run if budget allows".

### 2026-09-08, amendment A1 to pre-registration v1: gradient-probe checkpoints and design (before any pre-registered run started)

- Planned (v1, H1(b)): the probe at the base model and the gate-1 checkpoints 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100; independent groups resampled as one rollout from each of eight schedules; per-rollout scores through a seeded random projection to 4096 dimensions; the mean-centered estimator of THEORY.md section 3b.
- Done (amendment, written before the probe exists and before any tier-1 result was seen): (1) gate 1 kept only checkpoints 80 and 100 (`save_total_limit` 2 at its training commit; DAILY_LOG day 6; known at the freeze and wrongly carried into the v1 text). Trajectory A is gate 1 at the base model, 80 and 100. Trajectory B is `t1-c2-paired-s1` at the base model, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 (all checkpoints kept; the seed is fixed here, before its launch). The two trajectories are reported separately and the H1(b) rule applies to each over its available checkpoints (outcome noise: every checkpoint; transition noise: a majority). No rerun of seed 0 is made to recreate the deleted checkpoints. (2) The independent design is resampled as independent training samples it: each sibling draws a schedule label with replacement, then a rollout conditional on it; the stratified design of the v1 text (one rollout of each of eight schedules) is reported alongside as a secondary comparator. (3) Both the mean-centered estimator and the implemented update (std-normalized advantages, DAPO token aggregation, the training tool mask) are computed; for outcome noise the corruption average is exact over the corruption masks of each clean group; the expected update's norm and direction and the reward-contrast variance are reported alongside the trace, and a smaller update norm is not read as a gain. (4) The projection is dropped: with G = 8 every quantity is an inner product of per-rollout LoRA gradients within a group plus running sums across groups, computed exactly in the LoRA parameter space, so no projection-accuracy check is needed.
- Affects: H1(b) only; every other hypothesis, rule and run is unchanged.

### 2026-09-08, clarification: exposure history of the test pool

- Planned (v1 section 2): the test pool is "generated after calibration and never used for any decision".
- Done: the phrase is too strong and is qualified here. Test-pool results were observed before the sweep in the v3 zero-shot calibration final sets (`calib-v3-qwen3.5-2b-c2r`), the gate-1 final sets (`gate1-c2-paired-s0`) and the DigitalOcean stack check's real-evaluation batch (32 test tasks). No parameter, threshold, mixture or decision rule was derived from them: T comes from the validation curve, the mixture was frozen before the test pool existed, the funded list was set from cost. They appear in the daily log as evidence that training works. Nothing is tuned from test results from here on; this entry is the register of exposures and is extended if another one happens.
- Affects: how the test pool is described in the paper (an exposure history rather than "never seen").

### 2026-09-08, infrastructure repairs after the second external review (no protocol change)

- Found (review of commit fafb27d): (1) the run register read only the root `episodes.jsonl` and `groups.jsonl`, so the register (and the primary AUC) of a resumed run would have covered its last attempt only; (2) resume picked the highest-numbered checkpoint directory without checking that its save had completed; (3) a refused invocation (provenance) wrote a fresh manifest and an empty trainer history into the run directory before failing; (4) a run id alone triggered archiving and resume, without comparing the spec or refusing a repeat of a completed run, and the launchers accepted a run id whose ledger row was still open; (5) exceptions inside the job body returned a FAILED result, so Modal's retry policy never applied to them, and the DigitalOcean chain had no retry at all; (6) the launchers refused a tree whose only changes were the ledger and launch registers written by the previous launch, which made a multi-host launch at one commit impossible; (7) the ledger row said "not pre-registered" unconditionally (fixed in the previous commit); (8) the MI300X training step time was a forecast from the evaluation-throughput ratio, and the DigitalOcean hourly rate was a repository constant rather than the account's billed rate.
- Done: lineage-aware register (`pairedrl.analysis.curves.lineage_records`: an attempt's records count up to the step the next attempt resumed from, and a phase repeated by a later attempt supersedes the earlier copy) with an explicit completeness block against the spec (`expected_eval_sizes`, `periodic_steps`, strict K x M diagnostic cardinality, group and rollout counts), per-attempt cost and a checkpoint inventory; `scripts/verify_run.py` as the acceptance check; complete-checkpoint selection (`trainer_state.json` plus adapter weights) with skipped directories recorded; refusals recorded under `refused/` without touching the run's state; spec comparison and a COMPLETE guard on relaunch (`extend_previous` marks the exploratory smoke tests of the resume path); ledger-based duplicate guard and a running-chain guard in the launchers, with `--relaunch` as the explicit override; one automatic re-raise on Modal (`reraise_attempts=1`: a failed first attempt is resumed once by the platform) and one immediate re-run per spec in the DigitalOcean chain; launch records exempt from the clean-tree check (the code that runs is still exactly HEAD). The production-shape MI300X smoke (`configs/smoke-do-train.json` then `smoke-do-resume.json`, exploratory) measures the training step time, checkpoint and resume behaviour before the DigitalOcean chains are sized, and the hourly rate the account bills is passed to every launch (`--usd-per-hour`) and recorded in the manifests; the 1.99 USD/h and 35 USD per run of the frozen section 5 are the freeze-time estimate.
- Affects: nothing pre-registered; the tier-1 launches follow these repairs.

### 2026-09-10, the second Modal stage launched two days late; C0 seed 0 cancelled on droplet A; chains re-planned at the measured MI300X cost

- Planned: the five post-freeze C2 runs launched together (Instruction 38, two stages an hour apart); droplet A runs t1-c4-paired-s0 then t1-c0-clean-s0; two C4 runs per droplet at about 17.8 h each.
- Done: (1) the stage-2 launch of Instruction 38 did not happen (the session ended after stage 1 was verified healthy; the day-8 log entry that reported the C2 runs as launched in two stages was wrong and is corrected in the day-10 entry), so the four remaining C2 runs start at this commit, about two days after seed 0; nothing about the runs changes and the launch register records the time. (2) t1-c4-paired-s0 took 24.1 h and 47.98 USD on the MI300X (534 s per step; evaluation phases 9.2 h), so a second run did not fit the account's remaining credit with margin. The queued t1-c0-clean-s0 on droplet A was cancelled (ledger row CANCELLED; the `do-a` register entry records whether it had begun training before the droplet was destroyed; an earlier annotation saying C0 would move to Modal is superseded, C0 stays on DigitalOcean), the C4 run was collected with its weights and verified, and the droplet was destroyed. The chains are re-planned at the measured cost, with both arms of each remaining C4 seed pair on one droplet in opposite order (DIGITALOCEAN.md, "Tier-1 chains").
- Affects: nothing pre-registered (every condition keeps its provider, model, data, schedules and rules); the launch times and hosts are in the registers.
